## Introduction

RSMS is a Raspberry Pi powered robotic camera arm. Its control system is based on remote access via SSH. The system can be adapted with a wide range of sensors other than, or alongside, its natural avatar as a remote-controlled camera arm. The system is designed to be integrated with [Scorpion](/blog/scorpion/); however, the system is operable independently as well.
